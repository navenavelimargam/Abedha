MRPL FINANCE, ACCOUNTS & AUDIT DEPARTMENT
GENERAL OPERATING PROCEDURE — FIN-GEN-001
Effective Date: 01-Jan-2025 | Review Cycle: Annual | Owner: Chief Financial Officer

1. DEPARTMENT SCOPE
The Finance, Accounts & Audit department is responsible for statutory and internal audit,
budgeting and variance control, treasury and cash flow management, taxation and statutory
compliance, accounts payable/receivable, and financial reporting to the Board and regulators.

2. BUDGETING AND VARIANCE CONTROL
2.1 Departmental budgets are prepared annually and reviewed quarterly against actuals.
2.2 OpEx variance beyond 8% and CapEx variance beyond 12% against approved budget must be
    explained in writing to Finance within 7 working days of quarter close.
2.3 Any single line-item variance exceeding INR 25 lakh is automatically flagged for review.
2.4 CapEx approval authority scales with amount: Department Head up to INR 10 lakh, General
    Manager (Finance) up to INR 1 crore, CFO and Board Investment Committee above INR 1 crore.

3. AUDIT (STATUTORY AND INTERNAL)
3.1 Statutory audit qualifications must receive a written departmental response within 15
    working days; qualifications repeated for two consecutive years escalate automatically
    to the Audit Committee of the Board.
3.2 Internal audit findings are classified High, Medium, or Low risk. High-risk findings
    require a remediation plan with a named owner and a 30-day target closure date.
3.3 All audit reports and working papers must be retained for a minimum of 8 years for
    statutory compliance purposes.

4. TREASURY AND CASH FLOW
4.1 Minimum cash runway to be maintained at all times is 45 days of average operating
    expenditure, reviewed monthly by Treasury.
4.2 Any forecast showing runway below 30 days is escalated to the CFO immediately.
4.3 All surplus cash beyond the operating buffer is to be parked only in board-approved
    instruments; no discretionary investment by individual finance staff is permitted.

5. TAXATION AND STATUTORY COMPLIANCE
5.1 GST reconciliation must be completed for every vendor invoice before payment release.
5.2 All statutory returns (GST, TDS, income tax) must be filed at least 3 working days
    before the regulatory due date to allow for correction of filing errors.
5.3 Any notice received from a tax or regulatory authority must be forwarded to the Head
    of Taxation within 24 hours of receipt.

6. ACCOUNTS PAYABLE AND RECEIVABLE
6.1 Standard vendor payment terms are Net 45 days from invoice receipt unless the signed
    contract specifies otherwise.
6.2 Payments overdue by more than 30 days beyond agreed terms are reported in the weekly
    AP ageing report to the Finance Controller.
6.3 Customer receivables overdue by more than 60 days require a formal follow-up note and
    are flagged in the monthly receivables review.

7. REPORTING AND ESCALATION
7.1 Monthly financial statements are to be closed and circulated internally within 5
    working days of month end.
7.2 Any matter with potential material impact on financial statements (fraud, major
    write-off, regulatory penalty) must be escalated to the CFO immediately, outside
    normal reporting cycles.
