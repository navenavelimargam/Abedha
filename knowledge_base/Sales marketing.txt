MRPL SALES, MARKETING & COMMERCIAL DEPARTMENT
GENERAL OPERATING PROCEDURE — SALES-GEN-001
Effective Date: 01-Apr-2025 | Review Cycle: Annual | Owner: Head of Sales & Marketing

1. DEPARTMENT SCOPE
This department covers regional and city-level sales, the dealer/distributor network,
pricing and revenue management, market research, digital and brand marketing, and customer
sentiment/feedback analysis.

2. SALES TARGETS AND REPORTING
2.1 Regional sales targets are set annually and reviewed quarterly against actuals by
    product line and by city/region.
2.2 Monthly regional sales data (city-wise, product-wise) must be submitted to the Sales
    Analytics team by the 3rd working day of the following month.
2.3 Any region showing more than 15% month-on-month decline must be flagged in the monthly
    review with a root-cause note from the Regional Sales Manager.

3. DEALER AND DISTRIBUTOR NETWORK
3.1 Dealer performance is reviewed quarterly against target set at the start of the fiscal
    year.
3.2 A dealer below 70% of quarterly target for two consecutive quarters is placed under a
    Performance Improvement Plan by the Regional Sales Manager.
3.3 A dealer below 50% of target for two consecutive quarters is escalated to the Zonal
    Head for contract review, which may include territory reassignment.

4. PRICING AND DISCOUNT APPROVAL
4.1 Discounts up to 3% off list price may be approved directly by the Regional Sales
    Manager.
4.2 Discounts of 3-7% require Zonal Head approval with written justification.
4.3 Discounts above 7% require Head of Sales approval and are logged in the quarterly
    discount exception report; no discount above 15% is permitted without CFO sign-off.

5. MARKETING AND CAMPAIGN MANAGEMENT
5.1 Campaigns are evaluated on ROI (return on ad spend) at the 30-day and 90-day marks
    post-launch, reconciled against actual sales conversion, not platform-reported clicks
    alone.
5.2 A campaign with ROI below 1.2x at the 90-day mark is flagged for review and requires
    justification before further budget is released.
5.3 All external-facing marketing material must be reviewed by Corporate Communications
    before publication for brand and regulatory compliance.

6. MARKET RESEARCH AND COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE
6.1 Competitor and market study reports are to be summarized and circulated to the
    Commercial Committee on a quarterly basis.
6.2 Any significant competitor pricing move (greater than 5% price change) must be flagged
    to the Head of Sales within 48 hours of being identified.

7. CUSTOMER FEEDBACK AND SENTIMENT
7.1 Customer feedback and survey data is reviewed monthly to identify recurring themes and
    complaint categories.
7.2 Any complaint theme affecting more than 10% of respondents in a given month is escalated
    to the relevant product/service owner for a corrective action plan.

END OF SALES-GEN-001
