MRPL INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY DEPARTMENT
GENERAL OPERATING PROCEDURE — IT-GEN-001
Effective Date: 01-Jun-2025 | Review Cycle: Annual | Owner: Head of IT Infrastructure

1. DEPARTMENT SCOPE
This department covers application development, DevOps/infrastructure, cybersecurity and
incident response, customer/employee support (helpdesk), code review, and internal data
and analytics requests.

2. INCIDENT SEVERITY AND RESPONSE
2.1 Severity 1 (Critical, business-critical system down): 15-minute response, 4-hour
    resolution target.
2.2 Severity 2 (High, major functionality degraded): 1-hour response, 8 business-hour
    resolution target.
2.3 Severity 3 (Medium, single user/non-critical, workaround exists): 4 business-hour
    response, 3 business-day resolution target.
2.4 Severity 4 (Low, cosmetic/feature request): 1 business-day response, 10 business-day
    resolution target.
2.5 Any Severity 1 incident unresolved after 2 hours is escalated to the Head of IT
    Infrastructure with hourly status updates required until resolution.

3. CYBERSECURITY AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT
3.1 More than 5 failed login attempts on a single account within 10 minutes triggers an
    automatic account lock and a security alert to the SOC team.
3.2 Security-related incidents (suspected breach, malware, unauthorized access) bypass
    normal severity classification and are immediately escalated to the CISO.
3.3 Access reviews for all critical systems (ERP, DCS interface, financial systems) are
    conducted quarterly; any dormant account inactive for 90+ days is disabled automatically.

4. APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT AND CODE REVIEW
4.1 All production code changes require a peer-reviewed Pull Request before merge; no
    direct commits to the production branch are permitted.
4.2 Security-sensitive code changes (authentication, payment, data access) require a
    second reviewer with security review experience.
4.3 Automated test coverage must not decrease as a result of any merged change.

5. CHANGE MANAGEMENT AND DEPLOYMENT
5.1 All production deployments require a Change Request approved by the Change Advisory
    Board at least 48 hours in advance, except emergency security patches.
5.2 Any deployment causing a Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident within 24 hours of release
    must be rolled back immediately and reviewed at the next Change Advisory Board meeting.

6. SYSTEM UPTIME AND SLA TARGETS
6.1 Core business systems (ERP, email, DCS interface) carry a 99.5% monthly uptime target.
6.2 Non-critical internal tools carry a 98% monthly uptime target.
6.3 Any month falling below target requires a root-cause report to the IT Steering
    Committee within 5 working days of month end.

7. HELPDESK AND SUPPORT
7.1 Ticket categorization is reviewed monthly to identify the most common issue categories
    and drive proactive fixes.
7.2 Recurring ticket categories exceeding 20% of total monthly volume trigger a review for
    a permanent fix or self-service solution.

END OF IT-GEN-001
